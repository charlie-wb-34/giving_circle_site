Giving back, while enjoying half-priced drinks

This Fall, we're combining our love of a good happy hour with trying to give back to San Diego.

Growler Philanthropists is a giving circle — a group of people all pitching in a reasonable amount in order to make one large donation.

We'll be out every other Thursday at a bar/restaurant in San Diego, trying to collectively raise $5,000 for a good cause. For just $50, you can join and have a say in which organization receives the full donation!

HOW DOES THIS THING WORK?

01

Contribute $50

Become a member and join us on a Thursday at happy hour in San Diego. Location announced each week.

02

Nominate a cause

Have an organization you care about? Nominate it — they'll have the chance to receive the full $5,000.

03

Hear the case, cast your vote

Every member gets one vote. Nominations go up, we hear the case for each, and the room decides.

04

One gift goes out

The organization with the most votes receives the $5,000 donation, just in time for end-of-year giving.

WHO'S IN?

Finding 100 San Diegans this fall

Between September and December, we are aiming to find 100 awesome, fun, happy-hour-loving San Diegans to be part of the inaugural group of Growler Philanthropists. Come December, we'd love to drop off a $5,000 check to a deserving organization — and meet some great people along the way.

You might be wondering

What if my pick doesn't win?

It usually won't. You're trading control over one small donation for a say in something many times larger.

Where does the money sit?

In one account held by the organiser and reported to the group. Every dollar in and out is visible to members.

Can I give more?

Not into the pot. Equal stakes keep it a circle instead of a board. Give separately if you want to give more.

Is it tax-deductible?

The gift goes to a registered 501(c)(3), but the receipt is in the circle's name. Talk to your accountant.

Who started this?

For feedback (good or bad) and questions, reach out to Charlie Smith at charlie@charliecsmith.com.
