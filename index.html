How it works

1

Pledge $50

Commit your $50 via Grapevine. No money moves until pledges close, so you only give if we hit our season goal.

2

Nominate

Members nominate local nonprofits doing work they believe in. Any 501(c)(3) serving San Diego is eligible.

3

Vote together

Every pledging member gets one vote. The finalist with the most votes wins the full pooled gift — no split, no hedging.

4

Give as one

Grapevine collects pledges and sends one lump-sum donation to the winning organization on behalf of the circle.

Common questions

What if we don't hit the goal?

Pledges only process if the season reaches its member target. If we fall short, no one is charged.

Who can nominate?

Any pledging member can nominate a San Diego-serving 501(c)(3). One nomination per member per season.

Is my pledge tax-deductible?

Yes. Grapevine processes all donations and provides tax receipts directly to each member.

How does voting work?

Members who pledged receive a private ballot link. One vote per member. The org with the most votes receives the full pot.

Can I join mid-season?

Yes, until pledges close. After that you'll be on the list for the next season.

Who runs this?

The Growler Fund is organized by Charlie Smith, a San Diego resident. It's a community project, not a formal nonprofit.
