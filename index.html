How it works

01

Everyone puts in the same

Equal stakes, so everyone's vote weighs the same. The money sits in one pot until the group decides.

02

Anyone can nominate

Bring a nonprofit you know something about. Local, small, one you have volunteered for. Two sentences on why.

03

Pitch night

Three minutes each, out loud, in a room. This is the part people come back for. Questions after every pitch.

04

One gift goes out

Ranked vote, top pick takes the whole pot. Undivided money does more than a scatter of small donations.

Questions people ask

What if my pick doesn't win?

It usually won't. You are trading control over one small donation for a say in something many times larger.

Where does the money sit?

In one account held by the organizer and reported to the group. Every dollar in and out is visible to members.

Can I give more?

Not into the pot. Equal stakes keep it a circle instead of a board. Give separately if you want to give more.

Is it tax-deductible?

The gift goes to a registered 501(c)(3), but the receipt is in the circle's name. Talk to your accountant.
