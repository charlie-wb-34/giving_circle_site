Grab some post-work drinks and do some good

This Fall, we're combining our love of a good happy hour with giving back to San Diego.

We'll be out every other Thursday at a bar/restaurant in San Diego and want your help with two important goals

  • Create a community of professionals working in the area looking to expand their circle
  • Provide $5000 to a non-political and non-religious based nonprofit in San Diego

Growler Philanthropists is a Giving Circle. Everyone pitches in $50 so that as a group we can make one large donation to a single non-profit organization serving San Diego.

We'd love your help and look forward to meeting you!

HOW DOES THIS THING WORK?
01
Become a Member

Contribute $50, become a member and come join us on a Thursday at happy hour located in San Diego (location TBD)

02
Nominate an organization

Have a cause or organization you care about? Nominate it and they will have the chance to receive $5,000

03
We Vote!

Every member gets one vote. The nominations go up, we hear the case for each, and the members vote on which organization should receive the donation.

04
We deliver the $$$

The organization that most members votes for receives the $5000 donation, just in time for their End of Year Giving

You might be wondering

Who is this group for?

This was made by two professionals in San Diego that just wanted a reason to meet more regularly, meet more people, and do some good! If that sounds like you - then this group is for you

Where does the money sit?

The money is held by grapevine.org, a platform specifically designed for giving circles. They will handle the fund disbrusements and donations

What's required of members?

Only a $50 contribution. We'll plan on hosting a variety of happy hours and other types of events. We will share those out via email and instagram. No attendance is required!

What organizations can be nominated?

Any 501c3 nonprofit that isn't a single-cause political organization or religious-affiliated organization

Is it tax-deductible?

The gift is 100% tax-deductible and you'll receive a tax receipt upon completing your donation

What is Grapevine and should I trust it?

Grapevine is the donation processing platform. It is not associated with this group but we chose to use it to process the tax-deductible $50 donations

Who started this darn thing?

For feedback (good or bad) and questions, feel free to reach out to Charlie Smith at charlie@charliecsmith.com
