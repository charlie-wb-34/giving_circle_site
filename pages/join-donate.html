WHY BOTHER

Learn about the community

We'll have employees from local nonprofits share what they're working on and how they're helping San Diego.

Meet interesting people

A real simple way to meet people outside of your work who are also interested in making the city just a little bit better.

Explore new neighborhoods

We plan on trying different venues across San Diego — a good reason to check out a part of town you haven't been to.

Small cost, big impact

Volunteering can be exhausting. This is a way to make a real impact, have some fun in the process, and give small but land big.

WHAT YOU GET

A membership, a vote, and the right to nominate a cause you care about.

Every other Thursday at a bar or restaurant in San Diego. Attendance optional — just send your fifty and vote if you can't make it.
