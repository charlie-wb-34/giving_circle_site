Why bother

01

Learn about interesting causes in the area

We'll have employees from local nonprofits stop by and share what they are working on and how they are helping San Diego

02

Meet interesting people

Real simple way to meet people outside of your work and who are also interested in making the city just a little bit better. 

03

Chance to check out new restaurants/bars

We plan on trying different areas and seeing different neighborhoods

04

Chance to give small, but make a large impact

Volunteering can be exhausting and making a small donation from home just isn't the same! This is a way to make an impact and have some fun in the process. 

05

The more the merrier!

The more people that join, the more interesting this group becomes and the greater impact we can make!

What signing up gets you
A vote

One member, one vote on which organization receives the full donation.

A nomination

Put forward a cause you care about and make the case for it.

A standing invitation

Every other Thursday at a bar or restaurant in San Diego. Attendance optional.

Join the circle

$50 gets you a membership, a vote, and a nomination. The donation is processed by Grapevine and is 100% tax-deductible.
