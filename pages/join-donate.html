Before you pledge

Is $50 really all it takes?

Yes. One flat pledge per season, per person. No upsells, no recurring charges, no suggested "upgrade" tiers.

What does my pledge unlock?

A vote. Every pledging member gets one ballot when voting opens. Your say is equal to every other member's.

When is my card charged?

After pledges close and we confirm the season is live. If the season falls short of the member minimum, you are never charged.

Will I get a tax receipt?

Yes. Grapevine sends each member a receipt for their $50 contribution. Keep it for your tax records.

Can I pledge for someone else?

No. Each pledge must be from the person who intends to vote. Proxy pledges aren't allowed — it would skew the vote.

What if I change my mind?

You can cancel your pledge on Grapevine before pledges close. After that, the commitment is final for the season.
