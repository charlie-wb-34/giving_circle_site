Event FAQ

Do I need to be a member to attend?

Kickoff drinks and the Gift Party are open to everyone. Nominations Night and voting are for pledging members only.

Are events free?

Yes. The Growler Fund doesn't charge for events. Buy a drink, tip well, show up — that's the entry fee.

How do I get notified?

Sign up for updates on the Join page and you'll get event announcements by email.

Can I bring a guest?

Absolutely, to any open event. A guest who wants to pledge their own $50 and vote can sign up on Grapevine.
