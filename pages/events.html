Events

Every other Thursday, September through December

We are still finding locations, and are open to suggestions! We want to accommodate as many of our members as possible. We also plan on inviting employees and representatives from nonprofits so we can learn more about their causes and how they help the area. 

The two dates that matter
Nov 19
Members vote

Nominations go up, we hear the case for each, and the room decides together.

Dec 3
The check

One lucky organization receives the full donation, in time for end of year giving.
