What is this group?

Growler Philanthropists is a giving circle — a group of San Diegans combining a love of happy hour with giving back to the community.

Every member chips in $50. We meet up every other Thursday this fall, and by December we hand one deserving San Diego nonprofit a $5,000 check.

How it works
01
Become a member

Contribute $50 and you're in. Come out to as many or as few happy hours as you like.

02
Nominate a nonprofit

Know a cause worth supporting? Nominate any 501c3 serving San Diego and make the case to the group.

03
The group votes

Every member gets one vote. Nominations go up in November, we hear the pitches, and the room decides.

04
One big check

In December, the winning organization receives $5,000 — right in time for end-of-year giving.

And along the way

We meet up every other Thursday to check out new areas around San Diego and help wind down after work. As it grows, we can expand the types of events we're offering and are always looking for feedback!
